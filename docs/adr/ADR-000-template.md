# ADR-NNN: Short imperative title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted YYYY-MM-DD | Superseded by ADR-NNN

## Context

What forces are at play? What problem are we solving, and what constraints
(technical, product, portfolio) shape the answer? Write this so a reader who
was not in the room understands why a decision was needed at all.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly in one or two sentences, followed by the
concrete shape it takes in this repo (modules, config, CI gates).

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are explicitly
accepting as a cost. Honest tradeoffs only — an ADR with no downsides is
marketing, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, with the reason it lost. One line each is fine;
the point is showing the decision was a choice, not a default.
